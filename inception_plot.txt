Inception follows Dom Cobb, a professional thief who steals secrets from deep within the subconscious during the dream state. He is offered a chance to have his criminal record erased and return to his children if he can perform 'inception'—planting an idea in a target's mind instead of stealing one. Cobb assembles a team to navigate multiple layers of dreams, where the stakes are high, time perception is distorted, and the lines between reality and dream become dangerously blurred.
